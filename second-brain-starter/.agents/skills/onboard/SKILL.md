Read `.claude/commands/onboard.md` in this folder and follow it exactly. It is the canonical onboarding interview, shared by every supported app.

Wherever it mentions the onboard or briefing command, give the form for the app you're in: `$onboard` and `$briefing` in ChatGPT, `/onboard` and `/briefing` in Antigravity. Saying "continue onboarding" or "do my briefing" also works.
