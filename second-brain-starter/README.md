# Second Brain Starter Kit

Workshop: **"Build a Second Brain with Claude"** — Distant Proximity

This folder becomes your second brain: a set of plain notes about your projects and the people in your life, kept up to date by Claude. **You are the manager; Claude is the executor.**

## Before the workshop

1. A **paid Claude plan** (Pro or Max).
2. The **Claude desktop app** (Mac or Windows): https://claude.ai/download — sign in.
3. **Obsidian** (free): https://obsidian.md — for reading your notes.
4. Unzip this folder somewhere easy to find, e.g. `Documents/second-brain`.

## Getting started

1. Open the Claude desktop app and switch to the **Code** tab.
2. Choose this folder (`second-brain`) as the project folder.
3. Type **`/onboard`** and press Enter. Claude will interview you, a few questions at a time, and create your notes as you go. It takes about 45–75 minutes; you can stop any time and type `/onboard` again later to continue.
4. In Obsidian: **Open folder as vault** → choose the same folder, to browse your notes and the links between them.

## Every day after that

- Type **`/briefing`** each morning for your daily briefing.
- Tell Claude when something changes ("I spoke to Anna — she'll send the quote by Friday"). Anything you type, paste or attach is considered for your notes: Claude files it in the right project or person note and tells you what it changed. Changes to the rulebook are only proposed, never made silently.

## What's in here

| File | Purpose |
|------|---------|
| `CLAUDE.md` | Claude's standing instructions — read every session |
| `.claude/commands/onboard.md` | The `/onboard` setup interview |
| `.claude/commands/briefing.md` | The `/briefing` daily briefing |

Everything else (`about-me.md`, `rulebook.md`, `projects/`, `people/`, `daily/`, `input/`, `output/`) is created during onboarding. Put files you want Claude to read in `input/`; anything Claude creates for you that isn't a note (PDFs, images, slides, drafts to publish or forward) lands in `output/`, in a subfolder per project where it fits.

## Privacy

Your notes are plain files on your own computer. Claude reads them when you work with it in this folder. Claude will ask before storing anything sensitive, and never sends, deletes or spends anything without your approval.
